What this is

A philavery: a personal hoard of words, thirty-eight of them, collected rather than compiled. A gazetteer is the index to a map — the alphabetical list that only makes sense alongside the terrain it refers to — and that is the shape of this site. The plain view is the index; the field is the terrain.

The single rule the site exists to enforce: a word's behaviour is derived from its definition, and the definition is what arrives after the behaviour has already told it. Phlegmatic does nothing at all, and that is its implementation, not an omission. Nothing here is animated because animation is nice.

The field, and how it is walked

Words are nodes in a two-dimensional field, positioned by a force layout over two axes: language of origin runs across, semantic cluster runs down. One word holds a bordered exclusion zone no other word may enter; one word never comes to rest; one anchors the lowest point with the water column above it. A cross-hair — the walker, the one red mark on the page — moves between adjacent words. Arrow keys move it, Enter activates the word under it, Escape closes the definition panel, and clicking any word walks there. Whether the walker is driven or drifts is decided in the field itself, at the entry whose meaning is the holding of that choice.

What the numbers mean

The coverage figure inside the entry for ergodic is visited words over total words, as a proportion; steps counts arrivals, including repeats. The gap between the two is the point: the walk is much longer than the map. Both persist in this browser's localStorage, so the figure is a record of strolling, not a session counter. The dose count on Antidotum Mithridatium persists the same way, and the bearing on azimuthally does not survive a reload, which is what a session deserves.

What is exact and what is idealised

The attestation dates behind the stratigraphic section are century-level placeholders, not yet verified against the OED; the section says so where it is drawn. Provenance is recorded for one word only — mimeomia — and reads TODO everywhere else, which is stated rather than hidden because the source field is the one that will matter most in ten years. Musique concrète shows the waveform of a recording, rendered and not played; the audio dependency was judged not worth carrying. And atavistic reverts to the first commit of the house style's own tokens, whose palette turns out never to have changed — so the reversion is carried by the ancestral typefaces kept inside the token file's own fallback stacks.
